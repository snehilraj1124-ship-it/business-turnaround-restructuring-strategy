# Business Turnaround & Restructuring Strategy — NovaRetail

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive **Business Turnaround & Restructuring Strategy** for NovaRetail, a fictional Indian omnichannel retailer experiencing declining revenue, negative EBITDA, margin compression, excess inventory, high fixed costs and weak store productivity.

The project demonstrates how management can diagnose business deterioration, stabilize cash flow, restructure operations and create a path toward sustainable profitability.

> **Core Question:** How can an underperforming business restore profitability and cash generation without destroying the capabilities required for future growth?

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The project aims to:

- Diagnose the causes of business underperformance
- Analyze revenue and EBITDA deterioration
- Identify profitability gaps
- Evaluate store-level performance
- Rationalize the store network
- Identify cost-reduction opportunities
- Optimize inventory
- Improve working capital
- Restore gross margins
- Improve pricing and markdown discipline
- Redesign procurement strategy
- Evaluate organizational restructuring
- Improve digital/omnichannel economics
- Protect valuable customers
- Prioritize turnaround initiatives
- Develop a cash stabilization plan
- Build a turnaround risk framework
- Create a 12-month implementation roadmap
- Develop a management KPI dashboard

---

# 🔄 Turnaround Philosophy

The project follows a structured turnaround sequence:

**Stabilize Cash → Fix Margin → Simplify Operations → Improve Productivity → Rebuild Growth**

The objective is not to reduce costs blindly.

The objective is to redesign the business so that it can generate sustainable cash flow and profitability.

---

# 🏢 Company Context

**NovaRetail** is a fictional Indian omnichannel retailer with:

- Physical retail stores
- Digital/online sales
- Multiple product categories
- Large inventory requirements
- Supplier relationships
- Customer loyalty programs

The company expanded its operating footprint faster than its underlying economics improved.

This resulted in:

- Revenue decline
- Gross-margin compression
- Rising operating costs
- Excess inventory
- Weak store productivity
- Higher working-capital requirements
- Negative EBITDA

# 🔍 Root Cause Analysis

The business deterioration is analyzed across five dimensions.

### 1. Commercial Problems

- Lower sales productivity
- Weak category performance
- Inconsistent pricing
- Excessive dependence on promotions

### 2. Margin Problems

- Gross-margin compression
- Discount leakage
- Product-mix issues
- Procurement inefficiencies

### 3. Operational Problems

- Excess inventory
- Underperforming stores
- Inefficient replenishment
- High fixed-cost structure

### 4. Cost Problems

- High rental costs
- Payroll pressure
- Corporate overhead
- Logistics inefficiencies

### 5. Cash Problems

- Capital tied up in inventory
- Increasing receivables
- Working-capital pressure
- Higher liquidity requirements

---

# 🏬 Store Network Rationalization

Store economics are analyzed by cluster rather than assuming every store has equal strategic value.

The framework considers:

- Annual sales
- Store EBITDA margin
- Lease-cost ratio
- Store productivity
- Location potential
- Strategic importance

Possible actions include:

**Invest → Optimize → Grow Selectively → Renegotiate → Exit/Relocate**

The objective is to create a smaller but more productive physical network.

---

# 💸 Cost Restructuring

The cost-reduction program evaluates:

- Rent & Lease
- Payroll
- Marketing
- Logistics
- Technology
- Corporate Overhead
- Other Operating Costs

Each cost category is evaluated using:

- Current cost
- Target cost
- Potential savings
- Strategic importance
- Execution priority

Cost reduction should protect capabilities that directly support revenue, customers and financial controls.

---

# 📦 Inventory Turnaround

Inventory is treated as a major cash-release opportunity.

The project analyzes inventory by category using:

- Inventory value
- Gross margin
- Inventory turns
- Working-capital opportunity
- Strategic action

Recommended actions include:

- SKU segmentation
- Better demand planning
- Replenishment optimization
- Supplier returns
- Controlled markdowns
- Slow-moving inventory liquidation
- Reduced inventory days

---

# 🔄 Working Capital Strategy

Working capital is managed across:

### Inventory

Reduce excess stock and improve inventory velocity.

### Receivables

Improve collection discipline and reduce outstanding balances.

### Payables

Negotiate commercially sustainable supplier payment terms.

### Net Working Capital

Reduce the amount of cash permanently tied up in operations.

The objective is to release cash without damaging customer service or supplier relationships.

---

# 🏷️ Pricing & Margin Recovery

Gross-margin recovery is a major part of the turnaround.

The strategy focuses on:

- Pricing architecture
- Product mix
- Procurement savings
- Controlled discounting
- Targeted markdowns
- Premium product mix
- Category-level profitability

Markdowns should primarily be used to clear uneconomic inventory rather than become a permanent sales strategy.

---

# 🤝 Procurement Transformation

Supplier negotiations should cover:

- Purchase price
- Payment terms
- Minimum order quantities
- Lead times
- Return rights
- Volume discounts
- Supplier concentration

Suppliers can be segmented into:

**Strategic → Critical → Negotiable → Replaceable**

The objective is to improve procurement economics while maintaining supply reliability.

---

# 👥 Organization Restructuring

The restructuring framework evaluates:

- Organizational layers
- Headcount productivity
- Role duplication
- Accountability
- Critical capabilities
- Management spans

The strategy protects critical talent in areas such as:

- Finance
- Operations
- Technology
- Merchandising
- Customer Experience

The objective is to create a leaner organization without removing capabilities required for recovery.

# 💻 Digital Turnaround

Digital operations are treated as both a growth and profitability lever.

Priority areas include:

- Conversion optimization
- Customer analytics
- Inventory visibility
- Personalized offers

The goal is to improve digital economics rather than chase online revenue alone.

# ❤️ Customer Retention

A turnaround should not sacrifice valuable customers in the pursuit of short-term cost savings.

Retention initiatives include:

- Loyalty programs
- Personalized CRM
- Targeted offers
- Repeat-purchase campaigns
- Customer service improvements
- High-value customer segmentation

The objective is to protect customer lifetime value while reducing acquisition dependence.

# 💵 90-Day Cash Stabilization Plan

The first 90 days focus on liquidity.

### Priority Actions

- Establish weekly cash reporting
- Freeze unnecessary discretionary spending
- Accelerate inventory liquidation
- Improve receivables collection
- Renegotiate supplier terms
- Review store leases
- Track restructuring costs
- Establish cash-war-room governance

Cash visibility becomes a weekly management responsibility.

---

# 📈 Turnaround Targets

The target operating model focuses on:

- Revenue recovery
- Gross-margin improvement
- Positive EBITDA
- Lower inventory days
- Smaller store footprint
- Higher employee productivity
- Stronger cash conversion

The turnaround should be considered successful only when improvements become sustainable rather than temporary.

---

# ⚠️ Risk Analysis

Key turnaround risks include:

### Execution Risk
Multiple initiatives may overwhelm management capacity.

### Employee Attrition
Restructuring can cause uncertainty and loss of critical talent.

### Customer Churn
Aggressive cost-cutting can damage customer experience.

### Supplier Resistance
Suppliers may reject new commercial terms.

### Brand Damage
Excessive markdowns can weaken brand perception.

### Liquidity Risk
Turnaround costs may temporarily increase cash requirements.

### Regulatory Risk
Restructuring must remain compliant with applicable requirements.

---

# 🏛️ Turnaround Governance

A dedicated **Turnaround PMO** should monitor:

- Weekly milestones
- EBITDA improvement
- Cash generation
- Inventory release
- Store actions
- Cost savings
- Restructuring expenses
- Customer metrics
- Employee metrics

Management should maintain a weekly turnaround scorecard.

---

# 🔮 Scenario Planning

The turnaround should be stress-tested under different conditions:

### Base Case
Moderate recovery with controlled restructuring.

### Recovery Case
Faster revenue recovery and successful cost initiatives.

### Downside Case
Slower sales recovery and higher restructuring costs.

### Severe Downside
Liquidity pressure combined with weak demand.

Management should define trigger points for additional cost and liquidity actions.

- Strengthen digital conversion
- Rebuild customer retention

- Scale profitable initiatives
- Strengthen customer economics
